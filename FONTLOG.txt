FONTLOG for Riottosa

This file provides detailed information on the Riottosa font software and its development history.

---

Riottosa is an open source variable display typeface developed for the communication activities of the collective OSA.

---

Version 1.0 — May 2026

- Initial public release
- Variable font with width and weight axes
- Four masters:
  Regular Condensed
  Bold Condensed
  Regular Wide
  Bold Wide
- Western European language support
- Released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
