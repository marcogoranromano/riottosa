Riottosa is an open-source variable display typeface inspired by the industrial heritage of Fontivegge, Perugia. Defined by bold geometric forms, strong horizontal contrast, and a unicase

structure, it draws from industrial lettering and protest graphics. Its two variable axes—Weight and Width—offer flexibility while preserving a strong visual identity.
